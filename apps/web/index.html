How to Use Signage Ctrl

Follow these simple steps to sync and control your displays in minutes.

1

Launch the TV Display

Open the app on your Smart TV or display browser, select TV User Mode, and enter a pairing PIN (e.g. 1111). The display will wait in full screen.

2

Connect your Remote

Open the app on your smartphone or browser. Log in with the same account, select Controller Mode, and choose the matching TV PIN (e.g. 1111).

3

Upload & Sync Media

Select dynamic slide layouts (Welcome boards, menus, promo banners) or upload custom images and video files. The linked TV updates automatically.

4

Manage Multiple TVs

Switch screens dynamically from your controller's TV list tab. Choose from PINs 1111 to 1010 to control up to 10 distinct displays.
